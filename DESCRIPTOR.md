# TipSync — Descriptor

**Lip sync sells the show. TipSync saves the business.**

---

You already know what lip sync is. It’s the hinge — mouth locked to the track, timing perfect, illusion intact. Without it, you’ve got a person in a dress. With it, you’ve got **drag**. TipSync takes that same discipline and applies it to the part nobody posts about: the **business** you’re running every time you step off that stage.

Texas doesn’t make it easy. Dallas on Friday, Houston on Sunday, a guest spot in San Antonio, a host gig you agreed to in a group chat at 2 a.m. while you were still in face. You’re not failing because you lack talent. You’re bleeding because your **gigs, money, and cast obligations** live in six different places — DMs, Venmo, Notes, screenshots, and whatever’s left in your head after last call.

**TipSync is the gig and revenue command center built for Texas drag performers** — especially the **head queen** who runs the show, collects the bag first, and pays her girls on Cash App when she gets to the Waffle House parking lot. We don’t touch your money. We don’t route to bank accounts. We don’t pretend to be Venmo. We give you **one pipeline** where every inquiry, every booked night, every guarantee, every tip, and every dollar you still owe Crystal is **synced to the show** — the same way your lips sync to the beat.

Open the app backstage. Thumb-friendly. Dark, glam, fast. **This week** shows you what’s coming across metros — Rose Room in Dallas, Eagle in Houston — so you don’t double-book your life. **Inbox** catches the inquiry before it dies in notifications. Tap once: venue, city, rate, role. When they confirm, it’s a **gig** — not a screenshot you’ll never find in March.

Show night ends. Money landed on **you** — guarantee, cash, three Venmos, mom handed you forty for the feature. TipSync doesn’t judge. It remembers. One card for Saturday: income in, cast on the ledger, **owed** or **paid** when you’ve sent it on your own apps. Dashboard screams what matters: **$275 cast still owed.** Not drama next Wednesday. Not “did I pay DJ Mike or the other Mike?” Just the truth, tied to **that** night, in **that** city.

You’re not buying a calendar. Calendars know *when*. TipSync knows **Houston pays slow**, **you hosted**, **$400 was the guarantee**, and **three people from Saturday aren’t whole yet**. Lip sync sells the fantasy. TipSync closes the books so you stop leaving money and friendships on the table because you’re a performer first and a bookkeeper never.

The name isn’t cute for cute’s sake. **Tip** sync. **Lip** sync. Same energy — precision, trust, showmanship applied to the operational chaos of an independent performance career across sprawling Texas metros. Tips land with the head queen. She distro’s on her QR codes. TipSync tracks the show. When the tip jar comes off the back burner, it’s **one queen, one link** — because that’s how the culture actually works.

Tax season? You’re not guessing between eight grand and eighteen grand while scrolling Venmo for lipstick emojis. Export **per-gig income** — date, city, venue, what you logged. Not a CPA in a box. **Clean input** for you or whoever you pray to in April.

Wardrobe counts, receipt snaps, aggregated fan QR — all on the roadmap. **Version one is the wedge:** stop losing gigs in DMs, stop forgetting cast payouts, stop flying blind across DFW and I-45 with no single source of truth.

**TipSync** — for queens who run shows like a business and refuse to let the business run them into the ground.

*Putting on her heels. Sync your gigs to the show.*
