# TipSync — Revenue & monetization

**Principle:** Subscriptions are the engine. Everything else smooths cash flow without requiring TipSync to move money (v1).

**Positioning line**

> *Free to track the hustle. Pay when you run cast and file the bag.*

---

## What we are not monetizing (v1)

- **Payment processing** — no % on tips, no bank routing, no money transmission
- **Selling user data**
- **Display ads** in the app (off-brand, low yield in a niche community)

Copy and terms should repeat: *TipSync is not a payment processor. Performers receive tips and payouts via their own Venmo, Cash App, cash, etc.*

---

## Primary revenue: subscriptions

### Freemium (acquisition)

| Free tier | Paid unlock |
|-----------|-------------|
| Limited active gigs/mo (e.g. 3–5) | Unlimited gigs |
| Basic calendar + inquiry capture | **Cast ledger** (owed / paid per show) |
| Single city or limited venues | Multi-metro, venue memory |
| No export | **CSV / tax-prep income summary** |
| — | Inquiry templates, unpaid alerts |

Free tier must feel **useful** (real pain relief) but hit the wall when they run **cast shows** or need **export** — that’s the head-queen moment.

### Paid tiers (recommended)

| Tier | Price (draft) | Who | Includes |
|------|----------------|-----|----------|
| **Solo** | $8/mo | Single performer, few cast nights | Unlimited gigs, export, multi-city |
| **Head queen** | $12–15/mo | Hosts cast shows regularly | Cast ledger, unpaid dashboard, priority support |
| **Troupe** | $22–25/mo | Multiple cast slots, recurring troupe | Higher cast limit, shared show templates (future) |

**Annual:** $79–99/yr (~2 months free) — targets queens who think in “season” not month-to-month.

**Billing:** Monthly default; annual push at onboarding and every October (pre-tax-season).

## Ecosystem revenue (later, higher effort)

### Venue / booker side (B2B)

- Booker or venue account: post inquiries, see confirmed gigs
- **$29–99/mo per venue** or per booker seat
- **Risk:** chicken-and-egg; build only when performers pull bookers in
- **Wedge:** “Performers on TipSync get faster confirms”

### Featured performer / discovery (optional)

- Public profile: city, rate range, media reel
- **$5–20/mo** optional boost in booker search (when marketplace exists)
- Requires booker traffic; not v1

### Sponsorships & partnerships

| Type | Example | Model |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Brand sponsorship** | Wig, makeup, rhinestone retailers | Fixed fee per month / newsletter / “presented by” |
| **Affiliate (careful)** | Costume, makeup, streaming gear | Small CPA; must feel native, disclosed |
| **Service partners** | Drag-friendly CPA, business insurance | Referral fee or sponsored guide — no PII sold |

Community trust > short-term affiliate spam.

### Gig marketplace fee (high friction)

- 5–10% on bookings closed in-app
- Queens often resist; only consider if bookers pay or value is undeniable

## Revenue if payments become a big IF

*Not v1. Documented for future planning only.*

| Model | Typical range | Requirement |
|--------|----------------|-------------|
| **Tip platform fee** | 1–3% + processor cost | Stripe Connect, KYC, compliance |
| **Instant payout fee** | ~1% for same-day | Connect instant payout |
| **Premium tip page** | +$4/mo | Branded QR, analytics; free tier = basic link to her Venmo |

**Align with product rules:** default **head queen only** on fan checkout; cast splits stay off-platform unless Tier C split product is built.

See product brief: payments IF = legal + Connect + 3–6 months engineering.

## Pricing psychology (Texas head queen)

- She already pays **Venmo free**, **Cash App free** — subscription must answer: *“Did this save me one gig or one cast payout?”*
- **$12/mo** ≈ one forgotten $100 cast payout every 8 months — easy story
- **Head queen tier** should be the **default recommendation** in copy, not solo
- Avoid hidden fees; drag community is **word-of-mouth** — trust is the channel
